      First place as the carbon weight rises

      Ranking with carbon at this weight

      Why it is not weighted. Carbon was one of the six criteria the framework proposed. In Delphi Round 1 the panel rated its relevance in the platform decisions they had seen at a median of 2.5 of 5, below the cut-off of 3 fixed before the round. In the sustainability scenario, 5 of 6 panellists named it the scenario’s main emphasis.

      Read the order, not the grams. Absolute values depend on assumed workload, idle time and hardware. The warehouse is lower-carbon under the baseline assumptions, but a carefully run lakehouse (compute-optimised nodes, no idle clusters, an optimised engine) against larger-than-estimated warehouse nodes reverses that order. Differences between operating models rest on assumed coordination overheads that no source quantifies, so they are indeterminate.

      Performance matrix

      Coded from documented evidence on anchored 1–5 scales · select a cell

      Data: peer-reviewed studies and specifications, practitioner sources and vendor documentation, graded by tier (vendor documentation never counts alone), coded by the author on anchored 1–5 scales (coding kit v1.3). Cost for C3–C7 is derived from C1 and C2.

      1 5 = best: fastest, most AI-ready, most portable, cheapest, most secure Striped: derived cost class

      Method

      TOPSIS with vector normalisation; all five criteria are scored 1–5 with 5 as best. In the regulated-bank context, configurations below 3 on security are excluded before ranking. The ranking is cross-checked with PROMETHEE II and with min–max normalisation. Robustness is SMAA-2 with 10,000 draws. Carbon intensity is an exploratory add-on, not part of the weighting: a sixth, lower-is-better criterion on the estimate’s medians.

      Evidence

      Reference implementations: Snowflake Business Critical for the warehouse; Apache Iceberg with Spark and Trino for the lakehouse. Scores rest on specifications, studies and documentation, coded in October 2026.

      Status

      Panel weights come from Delphi Round 2, closed on 20 October 2026: four panellists, with two or three valid answers per scenario, so the weights are descriptive. Presets and sliders are illustrative. Thesis results are computed by the project's Python engine, which this page mirrors.
