## Coding notes

*Records requiring judgment.* #3 is a review of MCDM methods in software engineering with no decision problem of its own; coded `other` for method and `n/a` for weights. #30 and #31 derive design decisions from grey literature plus practitioner interviews without weighting anything — coded `literature + interviews` to preserve the distinction from author judgment. #9 and #17 derive weights from user feedback and sentiment mining respectively, which is neither author judgment nor expert elicitation; coded verbatim.

*The `unclear` entries.* Eight abstracts state that a fuzzy MCDM method was applied without naming the weight source. These must not be silently coded as author judgment — the count of `unclear` is itself reportable, and the Chapter 2 claim should be phrased over records where the source is determinable.

*Carbon coding is deliberately strict.* A record is marked CARB only where energy or emissions are treated as an evaluation dimension or measurement target. Papers mentioning sustainability as motivation are not counted.
